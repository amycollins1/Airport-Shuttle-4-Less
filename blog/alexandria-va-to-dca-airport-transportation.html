How Long Does It Take to Get from Alexandria to DCA?

Under normal traffic conditions, the drive from most Alexandria neighborhoods to DCA takes roughly 10 to 20 minutes. That number can climb during rush hour, special events on the Mall, or when the GW Parkway is congested, which is why AS4L drivers build in buffer time and monitor real-time traffic before every pickup. For domestic departures, we recommend a pickup roughly two hours before your flight; for international flights, closer to three.
