Best Transportation Options from Baltimore to Washington DC

The Baltimore to Washington DC corridor is one of the most traveled routes on the East Coast roughly 40 miles of highway and a mix of commuters, business travelers, tourists, and event-goers moving between two of America's most important cities every single day. With multiple transportation options available, the challenge is not finding a way to get there it is finding the right one for your situation.

Whether you need door-to-door luxury service, a reliable shared shuttle, or a quick public transit option, here are the best transportation companies and services for the Baltimore to Washington DC route in 2025.

Top Transportation Options from Baltimore to Washington DC

#1 Amtrak / MARC Train

Amtrak operates frequent rail service between Baltimore Penn Station and Washington Union Station, with the Acela Express making the trip in as little as 23 minutes and standard regional trains running the route in approximately 45 minutes. The MARC commuter rail Penn Line also runs this corridor on weekdays, offering budget-friendly fares for regular commuters. Trains run frequently throughout the day with dozens of daily departures, making this the most time-efficient public option when traffic is unpredictable on I-95.

  • Travel time: 23–60 minutes depending on service
  • Fares from $8 (MARC) to $26+ (Amtrak regional)
  • No surge pricing or traffic delays
  • Wi-Fi available on Amtrak Acela

★ Best for: Commuters, budget travelers, and anyone without heavy luggage

Known for early arrivals, flight tracking, and clean vehicles, KD Elite is a go-to for Maryland-based travelers who want a reliable private car experience. They handle both individual executive runs and family group trips, and are consistently praised for their driver professionalism and communication via multiple contact channels.

  • 15+ years operating the Baltimore-DC corridor
  • Covers BWI, DCA, and IAD airport transfers
  • Flight tracking and proactive communication
  • Family-friendly with luggage assistance

★ Best for: Maryland-based travelers needing reliable private airport transfers

#4 Greyhound & FlixBus

For budget-conscious travelers who do not need door-to-door service, Greyhound and FlixBus both operate scheduled coach bus routes between Baltimore and Washington DC with fares starting as low as $6–$15. Greyhound typically offers the highest frequency with up to 41 daily trips. FlixBus offers a comfortable, modern coach experience at competitive prices. Both depart from and arrive at fixed bus terminals, so you will need additional transportation to and from the stations.

  • Fares from $6–$20 per seat
  • Up to 41 daily departures (Greyhound)
  • Modern coaches with Wi-Fi (FlixBus)
  • Journey time approximately 1 hour

★ Best for: Budget travelers without luggage flexibility requirements

#5 Connect BBC

Connect is a full-service ground transportation company operating sedans, SUVs, vans, shuttles, and luxury buses across the Washington DC, Maryland, and Virginia region. They serve high-end corporate clients, shuttle for large organizations, and manage group departures for major events. Connect is a strong option for corporate accounts needing reliable multi-vehicle coordination and is the exclusive transportation provider for several DC metro area hotels.

  • Full fleet: sedans, SUVs, vans, limos, and coach buses up to 55 passengers
  • Exclusive hotel and conference center partner in DC metro
  • Covers BWI airport transportation
  • Ideal for large corporate and event groups

★ Best for: Corporate groups, hotel guests, and large-scale event transportation

#6 Uber Intercity

Uber's Intercity service connects Baltimore and Washington DC on demand through the standard Uber app. With average fares around $75 for a sedan and the ability to book up to 90 days in advance, Uber Intercity is a convenient option for travelers who prefer app-based booking and flexible scheduling. The service accommodates groups with XL vehicle options. Pricing is subject to surge and varies with demand, which can push costs significantly higher during peak hours and events.

  • On-demand booking via the Uber app
  • Average fare: ~$75 for a sedan
  • Advance scheduling up to 90 days
  • Subject to surge pricing

★ Best for: On-demand solo or small group travelers comfortable with variable pricing

#7 Zulk Transportation

Zulk Transportation is a family-owned local car service based in the Baltimore area, offering flat-rate airport and point-to-point rides in sedans, SUVs, limos, and passenger vans. Serving the Baltimore-DC-Virginia corridor with 24/7 availability, Zulk has earned strong reviews for its reliability, friendly drivers, and no-surprise pricing. A good choice for Baltimore-area residents looking for a locally rooted private car service.

  • Flat-rate pricing to Baltimore areas, DC, and Virginia
  • 24/7 availability
  • Sedans, SUVs, limos, and passenger vans

★ Best for: Baltimore-area residents wanting a locally trusted private car service
