BWI Sedan Service: Private Rides for Solo and Business Travelers

Our BWI sedan service is the simple, quiet alternative to waiting in a rideshare line. The Exclusive Sedan seats up to 3 passengers with 3 bags, which suits most work trips and weekend getaways. The Premium Sedan seats 2 passengers with 2 bags and gives executives a more refined ride to a meeting or hotel. The Luxury Mercedes S550 seats 3 passengers with 3 bags and is a popular pick for clients and VIP guests.

For arrivals that need to make a statement, the Mercedes Benz Maybach S680 and the Rolls Royce Phantom each seat 2 passengers. Call us to confirm luggage space on these two. Together, these vehicles make up our BWI airport car service and BWI black car service options.

BWI SUV Service: Room for Families, Luggage and Small Teams

When you have more people or more bags than a sedan can handle, step up to our BWI airport SUV service. The Exclusive SUV is our largest, with seats for 6 passengers and space for 6 bags, which works well for a family arriving with strollers and car seats. The Luxury SUV and Premium SUV each seat 5 passengers with 5 bags and give business teams and groups of friends extra room with a more upscale feel than a standard car. Let us know about car seats or extra luggage when you book so we send the right vehicle.

BWI Sprinter Van Service: One Vehicle for Your Whole Group

Splitting a group across several cars costs more and makes pickups harder. Our Sprinter vans keep everyone together with one pickup, one drop-off and one fare. The Mercedes Executive Sprinter seats 12 passengers with 12 bags and works well for corporate teams, tour groups and wedding parties. The Mercedes Limo Sprinter seats 13 passengers with 12 bags and adds a luxury touch. The Jet Sprinter seats 8 passengers with private-jet style comfort, and our standard Sprinter van is available for flexible group airport shuttle needs. Call for rates on the standard Sprinter, and to confirm luggage space on the Jet Sprinter.

BWI Limo Service and Party Bus: Make the Ride Part of the Event

Celebrating a wedding, prom, birthday or night out? Our BWI limo service includes the Lincoln MKT Limousine, which seats 8 passengers, and the Hummer Limo for larger celebrations. For bigger parties, we offer a 25-passenger party bus and a second standard party bus. Call for rates on the Hummer Limo and the standard party bus, and to confirm luggage space on the limousine and 25-passenger party bus. These vehicles are ideal when you want the ride to or from BWI to be part of the celebration.

BWI Mini Bus and Motor Coach: Transportation for Large Groups

For conferences, cruise parties, sports teams, school and church trips and family reunions, our largest vehicles keep it simple. The Executive Mini Bus seats 27 passengers with space for 27 bags. The 56-passenger motor coach seats 56 passengers and has space for 60 bags, making it the most cost-effective way to move a big group between BWI, hotels, event venues and the Baltimore cruise port. If your plans include several stops or a full day, ask about our bus charter and rental service.

Which BWI Airport Transportation Option Is Right for You?

If you are not sure which vehicle to book, use group size as your starting point. One to three travelers with normal luggage fit comfortably in a sedan. Four to six travelers, or families with bags and car seats, are best in an SUV. Groups of 8 to 13 should look at a Sprinter van. Parties of around 20 or more are better served by the 27-passenger mini bus or the 56-passenger motor coach. If you are traveling with more luggage than passengers, such as on a cruise, size up one vehicle. Our team can confirm the best match when you request a quote.

BWI Airport Transportation Services Available 24/7

Shared BWI Airport Shuttle

A shared ride is the budget-friendly choice for solo travelers and small parties who do not mind riding with other passengers headed the same way. It costs less than a private car and is more convenient than parking.

Private BWI Airport Car Service

A private sedan or SUV with your own chauffeur gives you privacy, a fixed pickup time and a direct route. It is a strong fit for business travelers, families and anyone who wants a calm start or finish to a trip. Read more about our Baltimore airport transportation.

Corporate, Point-to-Point and Hourly Service

We handle recurring corporate transportation for teams and clients, point-to-point trips between BWI and hotels or venues, and hourly chauffeur service when your day includes several stops.

Baltimore Cruise Port Transfers

Sailing from Baltimore? We provide transfers between BWI, hotels, homes and the Cruise Maryland Terminal at 2001 East McComas Street. Cruise bags are large, so tell us your group size and bag count and we will assign the right vehicle, from an SUV for a family to a mini bus or motor coach for a reunion or wedding group.

Popular BWI Routes: Baltimore, Washington DC, Annapolis and Beyond

Most of our BWI trips fall into a few common routes. BWI to Baltimore covers the Inner Harbor, Harbor East, Camden Yards, the Convention Center, Fells Point, hospitals and Penn Station. BWI to Washington DC is roughly 45 minutes depending on traffic, and is popular for business meetings, events and sightseeing. BWI to Annapolis is a favorite for weddings, Naval Academy visits and government travel; see our Annapolis shuttle service. We also serve Columbia, Towson, Ellicott City, Odenton, Laurel, Bowie and other nearby communities, and can quote any Maryland address on request.

Plan Your BWI Pickup or Drop-Off

A smooth airport transfer starts before you leave home. BWI’s official guidance on arrival times and TSA screening recommends arriving 2 hours before domestic flights and 3 hours before international flights, so schedule your departure pickup with that in mind and allow extra time during mornings, weekends and holidays.

  • Enter your airline and flight number when booking so we can track your arrival.
  • Add a mobile number that works after you land so your chauffeur can reach you.
  • Tell us about car seats, wheelchairs or extra luggage in advance.
  • Book early for holiday weekends, early-morning flights and cruise days.
