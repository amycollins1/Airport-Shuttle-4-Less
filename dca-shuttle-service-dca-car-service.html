Which DCA Airport Transportation Option Is Right for You?

If you are not sure which vehicle to book, use group size as your starting point. One to three travelers with normal luggage fit comfortably in a sedan. Four to six travelers, or families with bags and car seats, are best in an SUV. Groups of 8 to 13 should look at a Sprinter van. Parties of around 20 or more are better served by the 27-passenger mini bus or the 56-passenger motor coach. If you are traveling with more luggage than passengers, size up one vehicle. Our team can confirm the best match when you request a quote.

Popular DCA Routes: Washington DC, Virginia, Maryland & Beyond

Reagan National Airport's greatest advantage is its location — just 3 miles from downtown DC, minutes from Arlington and Alexandria, and with direct Metro access on the Blue and Yellow Lines. Most of our DCA trips fall into a few common corridors:

  • DCA to Washington DC — Capitol Hill, Georgetown, DuPont Circle, the National Mall, Penn Quarter, and all downtown hotels and federal buildings. Roughly 10–20 minutes depending on traffic.
  • DCA to Alexandria, VA — Old Town Alexandria, Carlyle District, and Landmark are minutes from the airport. Our most popular Northern Virginia route. See our Alexandria shuttle service.
  • DCA to Arlington, VA — Rosslyn, Crystal City, Pentagon City, and National Landing are all close to DCA. See our Arlington shuttle service.
  • DCA to Bethesda & Silver Spring, MD — NIH campuses, Bethesda business district, and Montgomery County destinations. See our Silver Spring shuttle service.
  • DCA to Baltimore — Inner Harbor, Harbor East, Camden Yards, Johns Hopkins, and Penn Station runs are approximately 45 minutes from Reagan National. See our full Baltimore airport transportation.
  • DCA to Annapolis & Beyond — Naval Academy visits, Anne Arundel County government travel, and Chesapeake Bay wedding venues. We also serve Fairfax, Springfield, Tysons Corner, Reston, McLean, and any DMV address on request.

Plan Your DCA Pickup or Drop-Off

A smooth Reagan National transfer starts before you leave home. DCA's official guidance on arrival times and TSA screening recommends arriving at least 90 minutes before domestic flights and 2–3 hours for international connections. During busy mornings, Friday afternoons, and holiday weekends, allow additional buffer time — DCA's proximity to DC means its access roads can back up quickly during peak hours.

  • Enter your airline and flight number when booking so we can track your DCA arrival in real time.
  • Add a mobile number that works after you land so your chauffeur can reach you immediately.
  • Tell us about car seats, wheelchairs, oversized equipment, or extra luggage in advance.
  • Book early for holiday weekends, early-morning DCA departures, and post-concert or event nights when demand is highest.
