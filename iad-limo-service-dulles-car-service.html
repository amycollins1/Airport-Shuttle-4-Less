IAD Sedan Service: Private Rides for Solo and Business Travelers

Our IAD sedan service is the calm alternative to a long taxi line or an unpredictable rideshare pickup. Why Book a Private Ride at Dulles Instead of Metro or Rideshare?

Dulles sits roughly 25 to 30 miles from downtown Washington, DC, so the ride into the city is a real trip, not a short hop. Metro’s Silver Line now serves the airport, and a ride from Metro Center takes just under an hour, but it means stairs, platform changes and carrying your own bags. A pre-booked chauffeur meets you after you land, handles the luggage and takes you door to door at a fare confirmed in advance. For families, groups, business travelers and anyone arriving on a long international flight, that convenience is usually worth it.

Which IAD Airport Transportation Option Is Right for You?

If you are not sure which vehicle to book, use group size as your starting point. One to three travelers with normal luggage fit comfortably in a sedan. Four to six travelers, or families with bags and car seats, are best in an SUV. Groups of 8 to 13 should look at a Sprinter van. Parties of around 20 or more are better served by the 27-passenger mini bus or the 56-passenger motor coach. If you are traveling with more luggage than passengers, size up one vehicle. Our team can confirm the best match when you request a quote.

Shared IAD Airport Shuttle

A shared ride is the budget-friendly choice for solo travelers and small parties who do not mind riding with other passengers headed to nearby destinations. It costs less than a private car and is more convenient than paying for airport parking or navigating public transit with luggage.

Private IAD Car Service

A private sedan or SUV with your own chauffeur gives you privacy, a fixed pickup time and a direct route. It is the strongest fit for business travelers, diplomats, government officials, families and anyone who wants a calm start or finish to a trip.

Corporate, Point-to-Point and Hourly Service

We handle recurring corporate transportation for executive teams and clients, point-to-point trips between Dulles and hotels, law firms, embassies, government offices and event venues, and hourly chauffeur service when your day includes several stops and you need a driver on standby.

IAD to Cruise Port Transfers

Flying into Dulles before a cruise? We provide door-to-door transfers from IAD to the Port of Baltimore cruise terminal, as well as to Cape Liberty in New Jersey for Norwegian, Royal Caribbean and Carnival sailings. Cruise bags are large, so tell us your group size and bag count and we will assign the right vehicle, from an SUV for a couple to a mini bus or motor coach for a group sailing together.

Popular IAD Routes: Washington DC, Virginia, Maryland & Beyond

Dulles is the main international gateway for the Washington region, and most of our IAD trips fall into a few common corridors:

  • IAD to Washington, DC: Capitol Hill, Georgetown, Dupont Circle, Union Station, the National Mall and downtown hotels, embassies and federal buildings. Allow about 45 to 60 minutes depending on traffic.
  • IAD to Reston, Herndon and Tysons: Reston Town Center, Herndon, Tysons Corner and the Dulles corridor business parks are close to the airport. Popular with corporate travelers.
  • IAD to Fairfax, Leesburg and Loudoun County: Fairfax, McLean, Ashburn, Leesburg and Virginia wine country, for business, weddings and weekend trips.
  • IAD to Arlington and Alexandria, VA: Rosslyn, Crystal City, Pentagon City and Old Town. See our Arlington shuttle service
  • IAD to Bethesda and Silver Spring, MD: NIH campuses, the Bethesda business district and Montgomery County. See our Silver Spring shuttle service.
  • IAD to Baltimore and BWI: Inner Harbor, Camden Yards, Johns Hopkins and the cruise terminal. See our BWI shuttle service and Baltimore airport transportation.
  • IAD to Annapolis and beyond: Naval Academy visits and Chesapeake Bay venues. See our Annapolis shuttle service. We also connect Dulles with DCA and PHL and can quote any DMV address on request.

Near the airport, the Smithsonian’s Udvar-Hazy Center and downtown Herndon are also popular stops for visitors with time between flights.

Plan Your IAD Pickup or Drop-Off

A smooth Dulles transfer starts before you leave home. Check the official Washington Dulles airport for current flight, terminal and security information. As a general rule of thumb, plan to arrive about 2 hours before a domestic flight and 3 hours before an international flight, and allow extra buffer during busy mornings, Friday afternoons and holiday weekends.

Wait Time, Meet and Greet and Curbside Pickup

  • Complimentary wait time: 45 minutes for domestic arrivals and 75 minutes for international arrivals, so you have time to deplane and collect your bags.
  • Meet and greet: your chauffeur waits at baggage claim for domestic arrivals, or in the international arrivals lobby outside customs and baggage claim for international arrivals, holding a name sign.
  • Curbside pickup and drop-off: your chauffeur will contact you by SMS or phone with specific instructions and helps carry your luggage.

Booking Tips

  • Enter your airline and flight number when booking so we can track your arrival in real time.
  • Add a mobile number that works after you land so your chauffeur can reach you.
  • Tell us about car seats, wheelchairs, oversized equipment or extra luggage in advance.
  • Book early for holiday weekends, early-morning departures and event nights when demand is highest.
