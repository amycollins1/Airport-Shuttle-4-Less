Montreal is a city built for arrivals that matter from the cobblestones of Old Montreal to the skyline of Downtown and the energy of the Quartier des Spectacles. Airport Shuttles 4 Less provides premium limo service in Montreal, QC, pairing a polished fleet with professional chauffeurs for weddings, corporate travel, nights out, and any occasion that calls for a luxury ride.

Whether you're planning a wedding party's grand entrance, moving executives between meetings, or booking a limo by the hour for a celebration that doesn't run on a fixed schedule, our team delivers dependable limo transportation built around your itinerary, not ours.

Our Montreal Limo Fleet

Our fleet includes stretch limousines and luxury sedans, each maintained to a spotless standard and paired with a chauffeur focused entirely on your comfort and timing. Every ride includes climate-controlled interiors, professional presentation, and a driver who knows how to navigate Montreal's downtown core, bridges, and highway traffic without cutting it close.

Wedding Limo Service

Your wedding day deserves transportation that matches the occasion. Our wedding limo service in Montreal covers the full day — from the bridal party's arrival at the ceremony to the reception send-off — with stretch limos sized for wedding parties, on-time chauffeurs, and flexible routing for photo stops across Old Montreal, Mount Royal, or your venue of choice. We coordinate timing directly with your wedding planner or coordinator so the transportation never becomes something you have to think about.

Corporate Limo Service

Built for businesses that need a polished, reliable ride for clients and executives, our corporate limo service covers meeting-to-meeting travel, client pickups, and executive transfers throughout Montreal. We offer consolidated billing, dedicated coordination for companies hosting visiting teams, and chauffeurs who understand the standard corporate travel demands punctual, professional, and unobtrusive.

Hourly Limo Service

Not every trip fits a single pickup and drop-off. Our hourly limo service puts a limo and chauffeur on standby for as long as your event runs nights out, city tours, multi-stop celebrations, or any itinerary that changes as the evening goes. Book the hours you need and let the driver handle the routing.

Limo Transportation for Every Occasion

Beyond weddings and corporate travel, our limo transportation covers anniversaries, birthdays, proms, wine tours, and milestone celebrations across Montreal. Every booking includes a dedicated chauffeur, flexible stop options, and clear pricing with no surge charges or hidden fees.
