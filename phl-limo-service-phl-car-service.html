PHL Sedan Service: Private Rides for Solo and Business Travelers

Our PHL sedan service is the quiet, predictable alternative to a taxi line or a rideshare pickup zone. The Exclusive Sedan seats up to 3 passengers with 3 bags, which suits most business trips and weekend getaways. The Premium Sedan seats 2 passengers with 2 bags and gives executives a more refined ride to a Center City meeting. The Luxury Mercedes S550 seats 3 passengers with 3 bags and is a popular choice for VIP guests and client pickups at PHL.

For arrivals that need to make a statement, the Mercedes Benz Maybach S680 and the Rolls Royce Phantom each seat 2 passengers. Call us to confirm luggage space on these two. Together, these vehicles make up our PHL car service and PHL black car service options.

PHL SUV Service: Room for Families, Luggage and Small Teams

When you have more people or more bags than a sedan can handle, step up to our PHL airport SUV service. The Exclusive SUV is our largest, with seats for 6 passengers and space for 6 bags, which works well for a family arriving at PHL with strollers and suitcases. The Luxury SUV and Premium SUV each seat 5 passengers with 5 bags and give business teams and groups of friends extra room and an upscale feel. Let us know about car seats or oversized luggage when you book so we send the right vehicle.

PHL Sprinter Van Service: One Vehicle for Your Whole Group

Splitting a group across several cars costs more and makes pickups harder. Our Sprinter vans keep everyone together with one pickup, one drop-off and one confirmed fare. The Mercedes Executive Sprinter seats 12 passengers with 12 bags and works well for corporate teams, sports teams and wedding parties. The Mercedes Limo Sprinter seats 13 passengers with 12 bags and adds a luxury cabin touch. The Jet Sprinter seats 8 passengers in private-jet style comfort. Call for rates on the standard Sprinter van and to confirm luggage space on the Jet Sprinter.

PHL Limo Service and Party Bus: Make the Ride Part of the Occasion

Celebrating a wedding, gala, birthday or a night out in Atlantic City? Our PHL limo service includes the Lincoln MKT Limousine, which seats 8 passengers, and the Hummer Limo for larger celebrations. For bigger parties, we offer a 25-passenger party bus and a standard party bus. Call for rates on the Hummer Limo and the standard party bus, and to confirm luggage space on the limousine and the 25-passenger party bus. See our party limousine service page for more.

PHL Mini Bus and Motor Coach: Transportation for Large Groups

For conferences, sports teams, school and church trips, tour groups and family reunions arriving at Philadelphia, our largest vehicles keep group logistics simple. The Executive Mini Bus seats 27 passengers with space for 27 bags. The 56-passenger motor coach seats 56 passengers with space for 60 bags, which makes it the most cost-effective way to move a large group from PHL to hotels, event venues or across the tri-state area. If your plans include several stops or a full day, ask about our bus charter and rental service.

Why Book a Private Ride at PHL Instead of SEPTA or Rideshare?

PHL is only about 8 miles from Center City, and SEPTA’s Airport Line takes roughly 25 minutes to get there, with trains running about every half hour. That works well for light travelers heading to a station-adjacent destination. For anyone with bags, a family, a group, an early-morning flight or a destination beyond Center City, such as King of Prussia, Cherry Hill, Wilmington or Atlantic City, a pre-booked chauffeur is simpler. You are met after you land, your luggage is handled, and you ride door to door at a fare confirmed in advance.

Which PHL Airport Transportation Option Is Right for You?

If you are not sure which vehicle to book, use group size as your starting point. One to three travelers with normal luggage fit comfortably in a sedan. Four to six travelers, or families with bags and car seats, are best in an SUV. Groups of 8 to 13 should look at a Sprinter van. Parties of around 20 or more are better served by the 27-passenger mini bus or the 56-passenger motor coach. If you are traveling with more luggage than passengers, size up one vehicle. Our team can confirm the best match when you request a quote.

Popular PHL Routes: Philadelphia, New Jersey, Delaware & Beyond

Philadelphia International is the main airport for the region, and most of our PHL trips fall into a few common corridors:

  • PHL to Center City Philadelphia: City Hall, the Pennsylvania Convention Center, Old City, Independence National Historical Park, Rittenhouse Square and downtown hotels. Allow about 15 to 20 minutes outside rush hour.
  • PHL to the stadium complex and South Philadelphia: Lincoln Financial Field and the sports complex for Eagles, Phillies and Flyers events, plus the Navy Yard.
  • PHL to King of Prussia, the Main Line and West Chester: upscale shopping, corporate campuses and Chester County conferences. Longwood Gardens is a popular day trip.
  • PHL to Cherry Hill and Camden, NJ: the Camden Waterfront, Adventure Aquarium and South Jersey business parks.
  • PHL to Wilmington, DE: hotels, corporate offices and events in Delaware.
  • PHL to Atlantic City, NJ: casinos, the convention center and weekend trips by sedan, limo or Sprinter van.
  • PHL to New York City: a long-distance private ride that avoids connecting trains with luggage.
  • PHL to Washington, DC and Baltimore: See our BWI shuttle service, IAD shuttle service and DCA shuttle service. We also provide Baltimore airport transportation.

We can quote any address in Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Delaware and the DMV on request.

Plan Your PHL Pickup or Drop-Off

A smooth Philadelphia transfer starts before you leave home. Check the official Philadelphia International Airport for current terminal, flight and security information. As a general rule of thumb, plan to arrive about 2 hours before a domestic flight and 3 hours before an international flight, and allow extra buffer on busy mornings, Fridays and holiday weekends.

Curbside Pickup, Drop-Off and Meet and Greet

  • Drop-off: your chauffeur takes you to the terminal for your airline.
  • Curbside pickup: after you land, your chauffeur contacts you by SMS with pickup instructions, meets you at the curb and helps with luggage.
  • Meet and greet: your chauffeur waits at baggage claim with a name sign, helps with your bags and escorts you to your vehicle. Greeter service is available for large groups on domestic and international arrivals and departures.

Booking Tips

  • Enter your airline and flight number when booking so we can track your arrival.
  • Add a mobile number that works after you land so your chauffeur can reach you.
  • Tell us about extra guests, car seats, wheelchairs or extra luggage as early as possible so the right vehicle is sent.
  • Book as soon as your flight is confirmed, especially for holiday weekends and early-morning departures.
