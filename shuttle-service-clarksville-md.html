Clarksville to BWI Airport Shuttle

Leaving from River Hill, Pointers Run, the Clarksville Pike corridor or the streets around Clarksville Commons? Your driver meets you at your door and takes you straight to your airline's departures curb. BWI is about 20 miles from Clarksville, and most trips take 25 to 35 minutes in normal traffic, usually via MD-32 and I-95. Your exact time depends on your address, the weather and the hour, so early-morning and rush-hour pickups are scheduled with extra cushion.

A private shuttle means no airport garage, no parking-lot bus and no luggage hauling on your own. One to three passengers ride in a Mercedes S550, Lincoln Exclusive Sedan or Premium Sedan. A family of five or six fits in a Cadillac Escalade, Premium SUV or Chevrolet Exclusive SUV, and a bigger party can share a 12-passenger Mercedes Executive Sprinter.

BWI to Clarksville: Pickups Timed to Your Landing

We also bring arriving passengers home from BWI to Clarksville. Enter your airline and flight number when you book, and dispatch schedules your pickup around your landing. Domestic flights include a 30-minute window after arrival and international flights include one hour, so you have time for baggage claim and customs.

If you want your driver waiting inside the terminal, add Meet & Greet for $35. A child seat is $20 and will be in the vehicle when you arrive. Request either one during booking.

Clarksville to Reagan National (DCA) and Dulles (IAD)

Reagan National and Dulles are longer trips from Clarksville: plan on roughly 60 to 75 minutes to DCA and 60 to 80 minutes to IAD in normal traffic, using US-29, I-95 and I-495. Our DCA shuttle and IAD limo service use private sedans, SUVs and 12-passenger Mercedes Executive Sprinters, and you see a flat rate before you confirm.

Drivers follow traffic as it develops and adjust the route to keep you on schedule. Federal contractors, business travelers and families on international itineraries often use the drive to catch up on email, take calls or rest before the flight.

How to Book Your Clarksville Ride

Booking takes about five minutes, and you see your full price before you pay.

  • Start on: visit the online reservation page. Choose a ride to the airport, a pickup from the airport, a point-to-point trip or an hourly charter. Larger groups can request a custom quote instead.
  • Add your trip details: Enter your Clarksville pickup address, destination, date, time and number of passengers. For airport trips, include your airline and flight number so we can time the pickup to your flight.
  • Select your vehicle: Pick a sedan, SUV, Sprinter or other vehicle that fits your group, then review the itemized fare and choose a gratuity of 15%, 18% or 20%.
  • Pay securely: We accept American Express, Visa, MasterCard, Discover and PayPal.
  • Check your inbox: Your email confirmation lists your pickup time and trip details, and your driver meets you at your door at that time.
